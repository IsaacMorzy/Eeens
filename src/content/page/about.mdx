      ## Lease terms

      - Standard industrial lease: 3-year minimum, KSH invoiced monthly, 2-month deposit, 1-month broker fee.
      - Residential sale: title transfer at sign-off, 6-month developer warranty on structure + mechanical systems.
      - Spec sheets (kVA, water, floor loading, clear height, parking bays) published on every industrial listing.
      - Viewing fee: zero.

      ## Locations

      - **Mlolongo** - Mombasa Road, KM 12 to KM 14 - warehouses, godowns, logistics.
      - **Syokimau** - Mombasa Road, KM 11 - light industrial, godowns.
      - **Baba Dogo** - Outering Road - business parks, commercial blocks.
      - **Thika** - Flame Tree Park Estate - 3-bedroom residential apartments.
